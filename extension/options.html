Saveform settings

Right-click any image, choose Save image as, then a format. To save several images from a page, click the Saveform button in the toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+S).

Images are converted on this device; Saveform makes no other network requests and collects nothing.

Saving

Use {name} (from the image address), {site}, {date}, {time}, {width}, {height}. Example: {site} {date} {name} → example.com 2026-11-20 photo.png.
