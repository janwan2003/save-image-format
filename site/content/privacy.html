Privacy policy

Applies to the Saveform website and the Saveform browser extension. Last updated: 2026-11-20.

The short version

Saveform does not collect, store or share any data about you or your images. Images are converted on your own device and never leave it. There are no analytics, no cookies, no accounts and no advertising.

The website

  • The converter pages decode and re-encode images inside your browser tab. Your files are not uploaded to any server.
  • HEIC photos are decoded by an open-source decoder (libheif, compiled to WebAssembly) that the page downloads from this site the first time you add a HEIC file. It runs inside your browser like the rest of the converter; the photo is not sent anywhere.
  • The site loads only its own files (pages, one stylesheet, scripts, icons). It loads nothing from other domains: no fonts, no analytics, no trackers, no embedded content.
  • The site sets no cookies and does not use local storage.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs to operate and secure the service; Saveform has no access to those logs. See GitHub’s own privacy statement for details.

The browser extension

  • When you choose a Saveform entry in the right-click menu, the extension reads the one image you clicked, converts it on your computer and hands the result to your browser’s normal download system.
  • When you click the Saveform button in the toolbar, the extension lists the images on the page you are viewing (their addresses and sizes) to show them to you. That list stays in the extension window and is discarded when it closes. Only the images you then choose to save are downloaded and converted.
  • To read an image, the extension fetches it from the address the page already uses for it. The previews in the toolbar window are loaded from those same addresses, without sending a referrer. It makes no other network requests: no analytics, no update pings of its own, no remote code.
  • Some image servers block other sites from reading their images. Only in that case does the extension ask for permission to access that particular site (or, when you save several images, those particular sites). You can decline, and you can remove a granted permission at any time on the browser’s extensions page.
  • Your settings (for example the JPG quality you prefer) are stored by the browser on your device. They are not sent anywhere.
  • The extension does not read your browsing history, the content of pages you visit, or anything other than the images on the page you asked it to save from.

Each permission the extension requests is explained in plain words on the extension page.

Children

Saveform collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. The full history is public in the project’s source repository.
